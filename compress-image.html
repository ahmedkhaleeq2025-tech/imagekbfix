How to reduce image size

  1. Choose your image.
  2. Adjust quality or enter a target size such as 200 KB.
  3. Click Compress Image.
  4. Download the result.
Your image is processed in your browser and is not intentionally uploaded to an ImageKBFix server.

Why compress an image?

Compression can help when an online form or website has a file-size limit, and can reduce storage and bandwidth use.
